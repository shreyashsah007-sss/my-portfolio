01 — About

Building things that actually work

I'm a 10th-grade student at Delhi Public School, Electronic City in Bengaluru. I build across hardware and the web — custom PCBs, IoT gadgets, and browser apps — and I run events for my local Hack Club.

I got into building because I wanted to see how things work end-to-end, not just use them. That pulled me from CircuitPython and KiCad into front-end code, and eventually into organising events. Most of what I make is public on GitHub.

When I'm not soldering or in a terminal, I'm usually planning the next Hack Club session.
